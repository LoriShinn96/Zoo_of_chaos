Penguins

>

Penguins are flightless birds that live mainly in the Southern Hemisphere.
They have black and white feathers.
They are excellent swimmers but cannot fly. They eat fish, krill and squid.

Lions

Lions are wild animals that live mainly in Africa.
They have golden fur and sharp teeth.
They hunt animals for food and live in groups called prides.
Lions are known as the kings of the jungle.

Elephants

Elephants are large animals with long trunks and big ears.
They live in forests and grasslands. They eat grass, leaves and fruits.
Elephants are intelligent and have excellent memories.
